# World land base geometry

`world-land.json` is Natural Earth's 1:110m land GeoJSON, downloaded on 2026-09-18 from the maintainer repository linked by Natural Earth:

- Dataset: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Maintainer: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
- Public-domain terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Unmodified geometry: WGS84 longitude/latitude coordinates, FeatureCollection containing 127 Polygon features. This coarse land outline is a frontend geographic base layer under online map tiles. It does not establish employer locations, national boundary claims, current recruiting status or job data. Existing source-record geographic centers are independently labelled as city, province or country references. Natural Earth attribution remains linked in the map alongside the online tile attribution.
